# Revisions of the Unity sample

The sample course has its own history. It is not part of Folio's version or changelog: those record how Folio works.
Each revision is a rebuild of `public/samples/unity.json` from the same generated course with more edits and media.

## 2026-10-04, revision 5
Every picture and clip opened against the step it sits under (four auditors, about 200 files). Seven pictures retaken,
one clip removed, one re-captioned, about ninety captions and descriptions rewritten. Weeks 11, 12 and 14 gained the
pictures they lacked. The example game keeps week 12's red hazard and controls label through weeks 13 and 14; a pickup
a capture script had moved in the week 13 and 14 project files is back in place. Unreferenced media removed.

## 2026-10-04, revision 4
Visual Studio Code pictured (the Extensions view before and after installing the Unity extension, scripts at the line
a step is about). Windows instructions checked against Microsoft's and Unity's documentation: the zip menu of current
Windows 11 and the Snipping Tool's save folder corrected.

## 2026-10-04, revision 3
The week 14 game built for macOS and run: a probe in the build photographed the game and pressed Esc, which closes it.
The week 13 hint, which sat on the player at a real window's size, is anchored to the bottom of the screen.

## 2026-10-04, revision 2
Each week's talk as a narrated slide video, read by a computer voice from its script. Weekly builds recorded as graded
complete or incomplete; the final project's three pieces count equally.

## 2026-10-04, revision 1
All fourteen weeks followed in Unity 6.6, with pictures, clips and project files from that run, and reviewed as a
beginner and as a whole course. Known gaps: no Windows pictures; Windows steps not run; no playtests with people.
