# My notes for the water cycle unit

A grade 5 teacher’s own planning notes for this unit, written for this sample course.

- Last year students mixed up evaporation and condensation. The "cold glass of ice water" demo helped: droplets form on the outside, not because the glass leaks.
- We have a classroom kettle (teacher use only), clear plastic cups, plastic wrap, ice, a lamp, zip bags and markers.
- Our school is in Phoenix, Arizona: summer monsoon storms are a good local example; most students have never seen snow fall.
- I want a "water cycle in a bag" model taped to the sunny window, checked over several days.
- Students should finish with a labeled diagram and a short paragraph explaining one path a water drop could take.
- Two students read well below grade level; one is a newcomer who speaks Spanish.
